\begin{abstract}
The $2$-local Hamiltonian problem is known to be $\QMA$-complete (Kempe, Kitaev, and Regev). When the terms of the Hamiltonian are restricted to pairwise commute, the problem is known to be contained in $\NP$ (Bravyi and Vyalyi). In this paper, we investigate the problem when the Hamiltonian terms pairwise $\eps$-almost commute (i.e. $\|[H_{i}, H_{j}]\| \leq \eps$).

We show that any 2-local qubit Hamiltonian $H$ with $m$ terms of operator norm at most $1$ each, where the commutator of every pair of terms is bounded by $\eps \leq 1$ in operator norm, can be rounded to a 2-local qubit Hamiltonian $H'$ with pairwise exactly \emph{commuting} terms such that $\| H' - H \| = O(m \sqrt[3]{\eps})$.

\noindent Consequently, the following are among the applications of our rounding technique.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item We prove that $\NP$ contains the promise problem of deciding whether a $2$-local qubit Hamiltonian with $m = \poly(n)$ terms that pairwise $\eps$-almost commute has a ground energy (minimum eigenvalue) $\leq a$ or the minimum eigenvalue is $\geq b$ when $\gamma - 96 \sqrt[3]{\eps} \geq \frac{1}{\poly(n)}$ where $\gamma = \frac{b-a}{m}$ is the relative promise gap.

\end{enumerate}

\end{abstract}
